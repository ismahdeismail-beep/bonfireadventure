## Assessment

Despite a "Blog" item in the header menu, there is **no real editorial content**:

- The posts are the theme's demo/starter set (including WordPress's own
  `hello-world`).
- The header "Blog" link doesn't even go to them — it points at `/contact-us`
  (F-14).

## Implication

If a blog/content strategy is wanted, it starts from zero and needs an owner,
a publishing cadence and a content model. There is nothing here to preserve. If
it is *not* wanted, the "Blog" nav item, the posts and the post sitemap should all
be removed rather than left pointing at the contact page.
